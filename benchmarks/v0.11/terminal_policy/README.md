# V0.11 Terminal Outcome Policy

The semantic-ambiguity outcome is intentionally narrow.

`NOT_CERTIFIED_SEMANTIC_AMBIGUITY` is used only when provenance, name
resolution, dependency/protocol resolution and available semantic evidence have
been exhausted and at least two incompatible source-consistent interpretations
still remain.

Missing evidence, unsupported constructs, verifier incompleteness, provenance
failure and execution failure retain their own preregistered outcomes.

No candidate may be dropped or replaced because it is difficult.

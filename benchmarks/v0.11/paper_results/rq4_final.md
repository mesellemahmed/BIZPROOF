# RQ4 — Certification Boundary

The frozen V0.11 evaluation terminalized 38 candidates as `NOT_CERTIFIED_UNSUPPORTED` and 2 as `NOT_CERTIFIED_SEMANTIC_AMBIGUITY`.

A candidate-level normalization of the frozen reason codes and reviewed reasons produced the following limitation taxonomy:

- `FRAMEWORK_OR_EXTERNAL_EFFECTS`: 19/38 (50.0%).
- `NON_SCALAR_OR_REFERENCE_SEMANTICS`: 10/38 (26.3%).
- `NESTED_OR_HIGHER_ORDER_LOGIC`: 3/38 (7.9%).
- `OTHER_FROZEN_SCOPE_LIMITATION`: 2/38 (5.3%).
- `STATEFUL_SIDE_EFFECTING_LOGIC`: 2/38 (5.3%).
- `BINDING_OR_DOMAIN_UNRESOLVED`: 1/38 (2.6%).
- `COMPLEX_CONTAINER_SEMANTICS`: 1/38 (2.6%).

These categories represent limitations of the frozen certification semantics, not errors in the source systems.

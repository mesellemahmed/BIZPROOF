# V0.9 Experimental Protocol — Symbolic Adapter Equivalence Certification

## Research question

Can BIZPROOF certify that a semantic adapter is not merely empirically consistent
with the external implementation, but symbolically equivalent to the selected locked
external semantic slice over the complete declared BVC domain?

## Source of symbolic semantics

The external formula is translated from the AST of the locked external source file.
Business decisions are not manually copied into the proof runner.

Supported external constructs for this experiment include:

- Boolean and integer constants;
- Boolean `not`, `and`, and `or`;
- integer `+`, `-`, and `*`;
- comparisons and chained comparisons;
- OpenFisca `not_` and two-argument `max_`;
- configured external entity lookups mapped to explicit BVC inputs;
- configured object attributes and method-call abstractions;
- local assignments;
- direct return expressions;
- simple `if`/return adapters compiled as symbolic ITE expressions.

## Proof obligation

For correct adapters:

`domain(x) AND external(x) != adapter(x)`

must be UNSAT.

For deliberately altered adapters, the same formula must be SAT and provide a concrete
model.

## Domains

- Apprenticeship-tax liability: complete Boolean domain.
- Housing-tax clamped subtraction: integer inputs in [0, 1,000,000].
- Oscar count predicates: `num_matches` in [0, 1000] and `required_count` in [1, 1000].

## Acceptance criteria

- all four correct adapters: `PROVED`;
- all four mutants: `DISPROVED`;
- every mutant proof contains a concrete model;
- zero translation or provenance failures;
- locked checkout commits equal the V0.6.2 lock;
- V0.8 remains the empirical source-executed preservation evidence.

## Scope limitation

This is a proof of equivalence for selected semantic slices, not a proof of the full
OpenFisca-France or Django-Oscar frameworks.

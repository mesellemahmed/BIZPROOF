# BIZPROOF V0.4 — Baseline Comparison Protocol

## Objective

Compare BIZPROOF with two established and methodologically distinct baselines on the fixed V0.3 business-rule benchmark.

The comparison focuses on **violation detection**, not on claiming that every method provides the same kind of evidence.

## Methods

### BIZPROOF

BIZPROOF receives the business contract and returns `PROVED`, `DISPROVED`, or `UNKNOWN`. A `DISPROVED` result must include a concretely replayed counterexample.

### Hypothesis

Hypothesis is used as a property-based testing baseline. The search domain exactly matches the declared benchmark domain. A mutant is detected when Hypothesis finds an input for which the implementation differs from the independent business oracle.

A `NoSuchExample` outcome is recorded as `NO_COUNTEREXAMPLE`, not as a proof.

Configuration:

- deterministic search (`derandomize=True`);
- no example database;
- bounded benchmark domains;
- 100 generated examples per case by default.

### CrossHair

CrossHair is used as an independent symbolic-execution baseline. Each candidate implementation is wrapped in an assert-based business contract with explicit domain preconditions. CrossHair analyzes the real Python function rather than BIZPROOF's AST-to-SMT encoding.

A zero-counterexample outcome is recorded as `NO_COUNTEREXAMPLE`, not as a proof.

Configuration:

- `analysis_kind=asserts`;
- 0.5 CPU seconds per condition by default;
- 5-second process guard per case.

## Benchmark

- 60 business rules;
- 3 domains: banking, e-commerce, insurance;
- 10 rule families;
- 1 correct implementation per rule;
- 4 non-equivalent semantic mutants per rule;
- 300 total cases;
- 240 mutant cases.

## Primary outcomes

For every method:

- mutant detection rate;
- correct-case false alarm rate;
- inconclusive/error count;
- runtime.

For BIZPROOF additionally:

- `PROVED` / `DISPROVED` / `UNKNOWN` semantics;
- replay validation inherited from V0.1.1–V0.3.

## Pairwise outcomes

For mutants, report:

- detected by both;
- detected only by BIZPROOF;
- detected only by the baseline;
- detected by neither;
- inconclusive baseline cases.

These counts support paired statistical analysis in the publication phase.

## Interpretation rule

`NO_COUNTEREXAMPLE` from Hypothesis or CrossHair must never be described as proof of correctness.

The purpose of V0.4 is to compare the ability to expose violations under explicit budgets while preserving the stronger evidence semantics of BIZPROOF.


## Metric semantics correction (v0.4.1)

Search-based baselines are evaluated as counterexample finders. A correct case on which
Hypothesis or CrossHair reports no counterexample is **inconclusive**, not formally proved.

Therefore:

- `mutant_detection_rate` compares counterexample detection on known mutants;
- `correct_false_alarm_rate` measures erroneous counterexamples on correct programs;
- `proof_rate_on_correct` is non-zero only for methods that produce a formal proof under
  the modeled semantics;
- `conclusive_case_rate` counts detected violations plus formally proved correct cases;
- generic `classification_accuracy` is intentionally not reported for search-based baselines.

This distinction prevents absence-of-evidence from being misreported as proof of correctness.

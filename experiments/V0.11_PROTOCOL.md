# V0.11 — External Generalization Cohort Protocol

## Objective

Evaluate BIZPROOF generalization on a prospectively frozen cohort of real business-rule
candidates that were not selected for V0.7–V0.10 certification.

## Frozen population

- Candidate source: `benchmarks/v0.6/results/candidates.jsonl`
- Candidate file SHA-256:
  `a8a706aa831427478b1b3e96bd7a60800865436a28ead2367b2c4edcdae8871f`
- Eligibility:
  - `classification == ADAPTATION_REQUIRED`
  - `business_relevance == STRONG`
  - not already represented by an existing V0.10 certificate
- Eligible population size: 730
- Eligible pool SHA-256:
  `33be87ff55ceb6ea0518a76dfcf0244fa4e103593cea2afb4cfb5dc2c9cc8ab3`

The V0.10 registry excludes three candidates from the eligible STRONG/ADAPTATION_REQUIRED
population. The fourth existing certificate is not a member of this eligible population,
so the eligible count moves from 733 to 730.

## Stratification

The cohort is stratified by external system and V0.6.2 adaptation complexity:

| Source | LOW | MEDIUM | HIGH |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| django_oscar | 32 | 251 | 52 |
| openfisca_france | 29 | 128 | 36 |
| pretix | 15 | 124 | 63 |

## Allocation

Exactly 10 candidates are selected from each of the nine strata, for a total of 90.

This is a deliberately balanced benchmark design. It is not a self-weighting sample.
Population-level aggregate estimates MUST therefore use the stored design weight
`N_h / 10` for each stratum.

## Deterministic selection

Each candidate receives the stable identifier:

`SHA256(source_id | file | enclosing_class | function | line_start | line_end | function_sha256)[:16]`

Selection score:

`SHA256("BIZPROOF-V0.11-20260926|" + candidate_id)`

Within each stratum, candidates are sorted lexicographically by selection score and the
first 10 are selected.

Business score, test evidence and documentation evidence are NOT used for selection.

## Evidence variable

Evidence is observed after selection and categorized as:

- `TEST_AND_DOC`
- `TEST_ONLY`
- `DOC_ONLY`
- `NONE`

Evidence availability is not a selection criterion.

## Pre-registered certification outcomes

Subsequent V0.11 execution will assign exactly one terminal outcome per sampled candidate:

- `CERTIFIED_A0`: certified without human semantic mapping or adapter rewriting.
- `CERTIFIED_A1`: certified with declarative source-to-BVC bindings only.
- `CERTIFIED_A2`: certified with an explicit human-authored scalar semantic adapter.
- `NOT_CERTIFIED_UNKNOWN`: sound analysis returns UNKNOWN.
- `NOT_CERTIFIED_UNSUPPORTED`: required source semantics exceed the supported subset.
- `NOT_CERTIFIED_PROVENANCE`: provenance/evidence integrity fails.
- `NOT_CERTIFIED_EXECUTION`: controlled source execution cannot be established.
- `NOT_CERTIFIED_SEMANTIC_AMBIGUITY`: no defensible business-semantic slice can be fixed
  without making an ungrounded interpretation.

A candidate may not be removed or replaced because it is difficult.

## Primary metrics

- weighted overall certification yield;
- certification yield by source;
- certification yield by LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH complexity;
- A0/A1/A2 assistance distribution;
- UNKNOWN and unsupported rates;
- provenance and execution failure rates.

Cell-level results are reported unweighted. Population-level aggregate results use
stratum design weights.

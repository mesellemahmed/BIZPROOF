# V0.10 Experimental Protocol — Evidence-Carrying Certification Pipeline

## Research question

Can BIZPROOF transform independently generated provenance, formal-verification,
source-executed preservation, and symbolic-equivalence evidence into a single
reproducible certificate whose integrity and cross-version consistency are
mechanically checkable?

## Certification layers

- **Layer P — Provenance**: locked external commit, source path and SHA-256.
- **Layer C — Contract verification (V0.7)**: correct adapter PROVED; controlled
  mutant DISPROVED; concrete replay validated.
- **Layer E — Executed preservation (V0.8)**: zero correct-adapter mismatches over
  the declared deterministic domain; controlled mutant produces mismatches.
- **Layer S — Symbolic equivalence (V0.9)**: negated equivalence UNSAT for the
  correct adapter and SAT for the controlled mutant.

## Certificate integrity

Every certificate is serialized to canonical JSON (sorted keys and compact
separators) and hashed with SHA-256. The digest is stored separately in the
certificate envelope and in the global index.

## Acceptance criteria

A rule is `CERTIFIED` only if every required layer passes and all cross-layer
provenance checks succeed. Missing, inconsistent or failed evidence results in
`FAILED`; the pipeline never upgrades incomplete evidence to a proof.

## Claim boundary

The certificate covers only the selected semantic slice, adapter and declared
BVC domain. No claim is made about complete external applications or framework-wide
correctness.
